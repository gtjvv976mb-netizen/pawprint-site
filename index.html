Pawprint

Dog care on your iPhone: health records, reminders, a daily plan from Georgia, and a photobook of your dog. Free, with no accounts, ads or tracking.

Keep each dog's vaccines, medications, weights and vet visits in one place, get a reminder when something's due, and ask Georgia, Pawprint's dog-care assistant, about food, care and safety. Her answers come from a library built into the app, and everything you add stays on your iPhone.
