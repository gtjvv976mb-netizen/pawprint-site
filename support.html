Pawprint Support

Pawprint is a free iPhone app for looking after your dogs: their profiles and health records, reminders, a daily plan from Georgia, a photobook and more. Everything stays on your iPhone.

Georgia isn't a vet

Georgia answers from a dog-care library built into the app. She can help you plan, check a food or prepare questions, but she can't examine your dog, diagnose anything or tell you a medicine dose.

If you think your dog is in danger, call your vet or an emergency vet now. If your dog may have eaten something poisonous, call your vet or a pet poison line straight away. Pawprint shows poison lines for some regions in Georgia's settings and on its emergency screens, and can open a search for an emergency vet in Apple Maps.

What Pawprint does

Dogs
Profiles with breed, birthday, food, allergies, vet and insurance details, plus weights, vaccines, medications, vet visits, reminders and a health journal.
Today
Georgia's daily plan for each dog: meals, walks, medicines and care, with an optional morning briefing notification.
Ask Georgia
Questions answered from Georgia's library, food and substance checks, and an emergency check.
Library
Dog-care articles and breed profiles, each with its sources.
Essentials
A short list of common products with an on-device check of certification and fit for your dog. No prices, ratings or sponsored placements.
Photobook
A book of your dog's photos you can share as a PDF or as a web page.
3D scans
A 3D model of your dog on iPhones that support Apple's Object Capture, or one you import.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Pawprint has no sign-in and no account. Open it and add your dog.

Where is my data kept? Does it sync to my other devices?

Everything you add is saved on your iPhone. Pawprint has no server and doesn't sync through iCloud, so your data isn't on your other devices.

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or to a computer, Pawprint's data is included in that backup like any other app's. See the Privacy Policy for the details.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty part of your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Pawprint. The "Next up" widget comes in small and medium sizes and shows your dogs' next care tasks, vaccines and follow-ups.

My reminders aren't showing up.
  • Check that notifications are on for Pawprint: open the Settings app, tap Notifications, then Pawprint.
  • Reminders for care tasks, vaccines and follow-ups arrive at 9 a.m. on the due date. Meal reminders arrive at the dog's meal times, if meal reminders are on for that dog.
  • Make sure the reminder itself is turned on in the dog's profile.
  • Dogs marked as in memory don't get reminders.
How do I turn on the morning briefing?

In the Today tab, tap Georgia settings (the gear), turn on Morning briefing and pick a time. It's one short note each morning with each dog's meals, walks and medicines.

What can I ask Siri?

Pawprint works with Siri and the Shortcuts app. Try:

  • "I fed Maple in Pawprint" (Mark as Fed)
  • "Log a walk in Pawprint"
  • "What's today's plan in Pawprint"
  • "Can my dog eat this in Pawprint"
  • "Ask Georgia in Pawprint"

Use your own dog's name in place of Maple.

Who is Georgia, and where do her answers come from?

Georgia is Pawprint's dog-care assistant. Her answers, food checks and daily plans come from a library of articles, substance checks and breed profiles built into the app, worked out on your iPhone. Articles in the Library show their sources.

Questions that sound like an emergency get her emergency guidance first, with buttons to call your vet.

What does "Reword answers with Apple Intelligence" do?

It's off by default. When you turn it on in Georgia settings, Apple's on-device model may reword some everyday answers to sound more personal. Georgia checks every reworded answer and uses her own words if anything looks wrong. It needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on.

On iOS versions that support it, a Deep thinking option then appears. It's also off by default; when on, harder questions may use Apple's Private Cloud Compute. The Privacy Policy explains what's sent.

How do I scan a food or treat label?

In the Today tab, tap Scan a label (or open Check a label from your dog's profile), then capture the label with the camera, choose a photo of it, or type the text. Pawprint reads the text on your iPhone and Georgia checks the ingredients, including against your dog's allergies and life stage if you pick a dog. You can save the calories per cup to that dog's feeding plan. If the camera won't start, allow camera access in the Settings app, or choose a photo instead.

Why can't I make a 3D scan?

3D capture uses Apple's Object Capture, which only some iPhones support. On other iPhones you can still import a 3D model file (.usdz) made elsewhere.

How do I share the photobook?

Open your dog's photobook, tap Share, then Share PDF. Pawprint makes the PDF on your iPhone and opens the share sheet, so you can save it to Files, AirDrop it, or send it however you like.

Can I share my dog's photos as a web page?

Yes. In your dog's photobook or profile, tap Share, then Share as web page. Choose all photos or only favourites, and whether to show the exact birthday or only the year. Pawprint makes one file named after your dog (for example Maple.html) on your iPhone. Send it however you like: anyone who gets the file can open it in a browser, with no app or account. Nothing is uploaded, and health records, vet, microchip and insurance details are never included. See an example dog page.

What is the sample dog?

When you first open Pawprint you can choose Look around with a sample dog. It's an ordinary dog profile: delete it from the Dogs tab whenever you like.

How do I delete my data?

Swipe left on a dog in the Dogs tab and confirm to delete that dog with all their records, photos and 3D scans. You can also delete single records, photos and scans. Deleting the Pawprint app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Does Pawprint cost anything or show ads?

No. Pawprint is free, with no in-app purchases, no ads and no tracking.
